About Infinity Hydraulics

Engineering the Future of Scrap & Material Handling

Established in 2019, INFINITY HYDRAULICS specializes in designing and manufacturing high-quality hydraulic equipment and material recycling systems. We serve industries across India with reliable, efficient, and cost-effective solutions.

Our product range includes: Hydraulic baling presses, lifting systems, hydraulic cylinders, shredders, and conveyors — each designed with a focus on performance, durability, and environmental responsibility.

With a dedicated team of expert engineers and skilled technicians, we offer customized solutions to meet the unique needs of every client. Our commitment to innovation, quality, and customer satisfaction drives us to deliver equipment that adds long-term value to your operations.

As the demand for industrial automation and waste management grows, we are ready to lead the way with cutting-edge technology and dependable service.

Why Choose Infinity Hydraulics?

  • Quality First: Premium materials, advanced manufacturing, and strict quality checks ensure top-notch machines.
  • Tailor-Made Solutions: Customized machines to fit your unique operational needs, space, and workflow.
  • Reliable Support: From service and spare parts to technical assistance — we support you long after delivery.

Every project we deliver is more than just a machine — it’s a promise of quality, reliability, and long-term support. INFINITY HYDRAULICS – We Minimize Your Efforts.
